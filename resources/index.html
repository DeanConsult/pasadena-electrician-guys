Home Safety
Electrical Safety Tips Every Homeowner Should Know

These simple habits can prevent fires, electrical shocks, and costly repairs down the road.

⚡ Don't Overload Outlets

Plugging too many devices into one outlet or power strip can trip breakers or — worse — start a fire. Use surge protectors with built-in circuit breakers, and avoid daisy-chaining extension cords.

🔌 Test Your GFCI Outlets Monthly

GFCI outlets (the ones with TEST/RESET buttons in kitchens and bathrooms) protect against shock near water. Press TEST once a month and click RESET to confirm they're working.

💡 Replace Flickering Lights Promptly

Flickering lights that persist after changing the bulb often signal a loose connection or wiring issue. Don't ignore it — call an electrician before it becomes a bigger problem.

🧯 Know Where Your Circuit Breaker Is

Make sure everyone in your household knows the location of your electrical panel and how to safely reset a tripped breaker. Label each breaker clearly for emergencies.

🌧️ Keep Water Away From Electricity

Never use electrical appliances near water. In outdoor areas, use weatherproof outlets with GFCI protection. If a device gets wet, unplug it and let it dry completely before using it again.

🔧 Schedule an Electrical Inspection

Homes older than 25 years — especially in Pasadena's older neighborhoods — may have outdated wiring. A licensed electrician can spot hazards before they become emergencies.

⚠️ When to Call an Electrician Immediately

If you notice burning smells from outlets or switches, discolored or warm switch plates, circuit breakers that trip repeatedly, sparks when plugging in devices, or buzzing sounds from walls — stop using the affected circuits and call us right away at 626-561-2717. These are signs of potentially dangerous conditions that need immediate professional attention.

Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I know if my electrical panel needs to be upgraded?
Signs your panel may need upgrading include frequent tripped breakers, a panel rated under 100 amps, a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel (both known safety hazards), or plans to add a major appliance like an EV charger or hot tub. We can assess your panel over the phone or with a quick on-site visit.
Do I need a permit for electrical work in Pasadena?
Most significant electrical work — panel upgrades, new circuits, major rewiring — requires a permit from the City of Pasadena. We handle the permit process for you. Permit requirements protect you as a homeowner, and unpermitted work can create problems when selling your home.
What is a CSLB license and why does it matter?
CSLB stands for California Contractors State License Board. A C-10 Electrical Contractor license (issued by the CSLB) means the electrician has passed background and competency checks, carries required insurance, and is legally authorized to perform electrical work in California. Always verify your electrician is CSLB licensed before hiring.
How much does an electrical panel upgrade cost?
Costs vary depending on amperage (100A vs. 200A), existing wiring condition, and permit fees. Most residential panel upgrades in the Pasadena area fall in the $1,500–$3,500 range. Call us at 626-561-2717 for a free phone estimate — we're upfront about pricing with no hidden fees.
Can you install an EV charger at my home?
Yes — we install Level 2 EV chargers (240V) for all major brands including Tesla, ChargePoint, JuiceBox, and others. Most homes in Pasadena can accommodate a Level 2 charger with a dedicated 50A circuit added to the existing panel. We handle all permits required by the City of Pasadena.
